## 📌 Overview
**Gallery** is a lightweight, blazing-fast, and modern gallery and file manager designed for Android. It prioritizes user privacy, sleek Material 3 design, and smooth performance while providing powerful photo editing, collage creation, video playback, and private storage features.

---

## 📱 Core Features & User Guide

### 1. 🗂️ Navigation & Browsing (4 Tabs)
- **All Media Tab:** Chronologically browse all photos, videos, and GIFs across your entire device with smooth thumbnail caching.
- **Folders Tab:** View albums and folders automatically categorized by directory (Camera, Screenshots, Downloads, WhatsApp, Videos, etc.).
- **Device Explorer Tab:** Deep file manager to navigate your internal storage and SD card tree hierarchy, create folders, and view hidden directories.
- **Vault Tab:** A secure, PIN-protected private safe to store sensitive photos and videos away from system media scanners.

---

### 2. 🎨 Collage Maker
- **Multi-Image Selection:** Pick anywhere between 2 to 9 images from your gallery to create customized photo collages.
- **Grid Layouts:** Automatically adapts layout templates based on the number of selected images.
- **Customization Options:**
  - **Aspect Ratios:** 1:1 (Square), 4:3 (Standard), 16:9 (Widescreen).
  - **Border & Spacing:** Adjust gap width and rounded corner radius.
  - **Background Colors:** Choose between sleek dark, crisp white, pastel colors, or vibrant gradients.
  - **Interactive Swap:** Tap two photos to instantly swap their grid positions.
- **High-Quality Export:** Saves the generated collage directly to your device gallery.

---

### 3. 📸 Image Viewer & Photo Editor
- **Pinch-to-Zoom & Pan:** Smooth high-resolution photo inspection.
- **Crop & Rotate:** Preset ratios (1:1, 4:3, 16:9, Freeform) and 90° rotation.
- **Wallpaper Setter:** Set any photo as Home Screen, Lock Screen, or Both directly within the app.
- **EXIF Inspector:** View camera model, aperture, shutter speed, ISO, file dimensions, timestamp, and storage path.
- **Favorite & Share:** Instant one-tap favorite star toggle and system share sheet integration.

---

### 4. 🎬 Video Player (ExoPlayer Media3)
- **Smooth Playback:** Hardware-accelerated playback supporting MP4, MKV, WebM, AVI, and 3GP formats.
- **Playback Speed:** Control speed from 0.5x, 1.0x (Normal), 1.25x, 1.5x, to 2.0x.
- **Aspect Ratio Control:** Switch between *Fit to Screen*, *Fill Screen (Crop)*, and *Zoom*.
- **Picture-in-Picture (PiP):** Minimize video into a floating window to multitask while continuing playback.

---

### 5. 🔒 Secure Private Vault
- **4-Digit PIN Security:** Protect your private photos and videos behind an encrypted PIN lock.
- **Zero Cloud Exposure:** Files never leave your device; they are isolated in hidden private app storage (`.vault`).
- **One-Tap Restore:** Seamlessly unlock and restore vault items back to their original gallery locations whenever needed.

---

### 6. 🧹 Storage Analyzer & Junk Cleaner
- **Storage Breakdown:** Visual categorization of storage usage (Images, Videos, Audio, Documents, APKs, Archives, and Other files).
- **Duplicate Finder:** Identify duplicate files consuming unnecessary device space.
- **Large Files Scanner:** Discover files larger than 50MB for quick cleanup.
- **Empty Folder Cleaner:** Clean residual empty folders left by uninstalled apps.

---

### 7. ⚡ Batch Multi-Selection Bar
- **Multi-Select Mode:** Long-press any photo or video to enter batch mode.
- **Batch Actions:**
  - 📤 **Share:** Send multiple files simultaneously.
  - 🗑️ **Delete:** Safely batch delete items with confirmation.
  - 📦 **Compress (ZIP):** Package selected files into a `.zip` archive.
  - 📂 **Move & Copy:** Transfer media across folders.
  - ⭐ **Batch Favorite:** Mark multiple items as favorites in one tap.
  - 🔒 **Protect:** Move multiple files into the Secure Vault.

---

### 8. ℹ️ About & Distribution Tools
- **3-Dot Overflow Menu:** Tapping the 3-dot icon in the top toolbar opens the **About** screen.
- **Direct APK Downloader:** Copies the production APK directly into your device's public `Downloads` directory with an *Open Downloads* notification action.
- **APK Sharing:** Generates a branded APK (`gallary-amit-bharat.apk`) and shares it directly via WhatsApp, Bluetooth, Telegram, or Drive.
- **Direct Feedback:** Sends pre-filled device and app diagnostics directly to developer support (`rooyssofttech2020@gmail.com`).
